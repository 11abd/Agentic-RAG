It does this by identifying the most semantically similar chunk from the database. This chunk then becomes the context for the prompt. When passed to the Large Language Model (LLM), it enables the system to provide a relevant answer within the given context. 6 Why do we need RAG? ●Expands Knowledge Base RAG accesses a vast external database, enriching its knowledge beyond initial training data ●Improves Accuracy Enhances response precision by integrating relevant, real-time information ●Adaptable Effectively handles novel and niche queries ●Increases Efﬁciency Streamlines information retrieval and generation process ●Versatile Applications Useful across various ﬁelds, from customer support to research Source: What Is Retrieval-Augmented Generation, aka RAG?

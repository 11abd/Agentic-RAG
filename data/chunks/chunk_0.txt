Just as humans cannot digest unlimited context, these models have a speciﬁc size limit for the content they can process. So, what about situations involving very large amounts of data? Consider a speciﬁc use case, such as a book. It's too large to pass the entire book as the context for the current prompt, so it needs to be divided before being stored in the database. This process is known as data chunking.
